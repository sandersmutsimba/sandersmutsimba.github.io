About

I specialise in safety management for high-end construction, including high-rises and hillside developments. I’ve run multi-subcontractor sites, led regulatory compliance and investigated incidents to find root causes. I am also a GIS and mapping expert. I use spatial analysis, such as buffer zones for fire extinguisher coverage, to turn safety data into maps that show where protection is missing. It started with my BSc in Geography and Environmental Science and my work as an environmental officer on a national carbon stocks project with WWF and the World Bank.

Subcontractor management system

How I bring every trade on a multi-subcontractor site under one safety standard.

  1. Master safety planOne set of guidelines and daily targets for primary and tier-two subcontractors.
  2. Inductions and toolbox talksBriefings in clear, local language so every crew knows the rules.
  3. Permits and exclusion zonesCrane lifts, steel erection and other overlapping high-risk tasks controlled by permit.
  4. Daily walkthroughs and weekly auditsTrade partners checked on site, not just on paper.
  5. Corrective action close-outEach trade held accountable for closing its findings.
  6. One emergency and incident planAll crews report and respond under a single plan.
