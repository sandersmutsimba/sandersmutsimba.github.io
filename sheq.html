Subcontractor management

How I bring every trade on a multi-subcontractor site under one safety standard.

  1. Master safety planOne set of guidelines and daily targets for primary and tier-two subcontractors.
  2. Inductions and toolbox talksBriefings in clear, local language so every crew knows the rules.
  3. Permits, exclusion zones and standardsCrane lifts, steel erection, excavation, hot works and confined space entry controlled by permit and exclusion zones, to ISO 45001 and NSSA requirements.
  4. Daily walkthroughs and weekly auditsTrade partners checked on site, not just on paper.
  5. Corrective action close-outEach trade is held accountable for closing its findings.
  6. One emergency planAll crews report incidents and respond under a single plan.
